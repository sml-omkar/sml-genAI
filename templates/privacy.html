Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

EthosAI (“the Assistant”) is an internal company assistant provided by Sanghvi Movers Limited (SML). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the Assistant, how it is used, and how long it is kept.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and company email address when you sign in to use the Assistant.
  • Chat messages — the questions you ask and the responses the Assistant provides.
  • Usage data — token consumption associated with your account, used to track and manage resource usage.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions accurately using company-approved documents.
  • To maintain conversation context so follow-up questions can be understood.
  • To monitor and manage system usage and security.
  • To improve the Assistant’s knowledge base and response quality.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. AI processing

To generate an answer, your question and relevant portions of the current conversation may be processed by a third-party AI language model (OpenAI). Document embeddings used for search are generated locally by our own infrastructure (Ollama) and are not sent to third-party services. Only content needed to answer your question is sent to the language model.

4. Data retention

  • Conversation history is stored temporarily and automatically expires after 24 hours of inactivity.
  • Policy knowledge built from uploaded company documents is retained until the document is removed by an administrator.
  • Uploaded documents and their searchable content are stored on company-controlled infrastructure.

5. Confidentiality

Do not enter personal or confidential information (such as passwords, financial data, or sensitive personal data) into the Assistant. The Assistant is intended for questions about company policies and procedures only.

6. Security

Access to the Assistant is restricted to registered employees, protected by authentication, role-based access control, and encrypted transmission. Answers are only generated when the requesting account is recognised and active in our user registry.

7. Your choices

You may stop using the Assistant at any time. To have data associated with your account reviewed or removed, please contact your system administrator.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the IT / HR administration team at Sanghvi Movers Limited.
